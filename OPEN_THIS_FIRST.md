# Open this first

GTS AI Research System Template (GTS-AIRST) is the reusable parent template for GTS domain-specialized AI-assisted research systems.

Start with these rules:

1. Model output is not authoritative evidence for external factual claims merely because it is generated, cited, fluent, or confident.
2. Recorded model output may be observational evidence when the model itself is the object of study and provenance is preserved.
3. Sources are not automatically evidence.
4. Evidence must support the exact claim being made.
5. Contradictory evidence must be preserved.
6. Uncertainty must not disappear during summarization.
7. Observation, inference, finding, recommendation, and decision remain distinct.
8. Search for what could prove the current hypothesis wrong.
9. Higher-consequence questions require stronger methods and often qualified human expertise.
10. Consequential decisions remain with the responsible operator or authority.

For ordinary research use, begin with 02_RESEARCH_KNOWLEDGE_TEMPLATE/00_INDEX_AND_ROUTING.md.

For a GTS domain derivative, do not copy main as an untracked fork. Use a qualified, exact parent release/tag/commit and DERIVATIVE_CONTRACT.md plus the derivative mutation manifest.
