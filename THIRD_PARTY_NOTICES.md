## Rules

Before third-party material becomes a production dependency or is redistributed with this project, record:
- item and version;
- rights holder;
- source;
- governing license or terms;
- whether redistribution is permitted;
- attribution/notice obligations;
- modification restrictions;
- commercial-use restrictions;
- patent/trademark implications where material;
- compatibility with the project's intended distribution.

Do not assume that material found online, generated by a tool, contained in a paper, copied from documentation, or published in a public repository is free to redistribute.
