# Examples

## Start here

[Your first research project: can this switch power eight cameras?](01_FIRST_RESEARCH_PROJECT.md)

This completed teaching example uses fictional source documents and specifications. It follows the existing project templates from the research question through evidence, claims, uncertainty, a recommendation, and a handoff.

It shows a rejected source and an unresolved alternative explanation. No real equipment approval or purchase decision is made.

## Challenge a research workflow

The [Adversarial Research Regression Corpus](ADVERSARIAL_RESEARCH_TEST_CORPUS.md) provides scenarios for checking how a workflow handles fabricated citations, copied claims, retracted sources, conflicting evidence, and other research failures.

## Planned examples

Additional examples will demonstrate the method at different rigor levels:

- simple consumer question;
- technical architecture comparison;
- scientific literature question;
- controversial factual claim;
- high-assurance example showing why specialist review and stronger methods are required.

Examples must show rejected sources, contradictory evidence, and uncertainty—not only successful conclusions.
