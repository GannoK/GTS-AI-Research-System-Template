# Legal and IP Governance

This directory contains governance policies and drafting templates for the GTS AI Research System Template (GTS-AIRST).

## Active governance

- `IP_OWNERSHIP_POLICY.md` — chain-of-title rules.
- `CONTRIBUTOR_POLICY.md` — contribution intake and rights requirements.
- `AI_ASSISTED_AUTHORSHIP_POLICY.md` — provenance and human-authorship records.

## Agreement templates

- `CONTRIBUTOR_IP_ASSIGNMENT_TEMPLATE.md`
- `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE_TEMPLATE.md`
- `ROYALTY_AND_REVENUE_SHARE_TEMPLATE.md`

Templates are **not executed agreements** merely because they exist in the repository. Required blanks, governing law, consideration, economics, signatures, and jurisdiction-specific provisions must be completed before use.

## Separation of legal functions

Keep these concepts separate:

1. **Ownership** — who owns copyrightable project material.
2. **License** — what permission a non-owner receives.
3. **Contribution assignment** — how contributor rights become project-owned.
4. **Confidentiality** — duties concerning nonpublic information.
5. **Compensation/royalties** — what money, if any, a person receives.

Do not use a repository license as a substitute for a signed contributor, contractor, commercial, royalty, or NDA agreement.
