# AI output and source authority

Model output is generated output, not authoritative evidence for external factual claims merely because it is fluent, confident, repeated, or accompanied by citations.

External factual content remains unverified until traced to appropriate evidence and source identity.

There is one important distinction: when the model itself is the object of study, a recorded model output can serve as observational evidence about that model/run if provenance is preserved, including the relevant model/provider identity, execution context, prompt/input, time, and transformations. That observational evidence still does not automatically establish external facts mentioned by the model.
