# Release Notes — v0.2.1

Date: 2026-10-10

## Purpose

v0.2.1 makes the GTS AI Research System Template easier to understand and use for a first research project. It adds beginner explanations and a worked example of the existing method.

## Beginner documentation

- The [README](README.md) explains what the kit provides, introduces common AI terms, and points readers to a first-project path.
- The [glossary](10_REFERENCE/01_GLOSSARY.md) expands AI, data, context, research, and repository terminology while retaining the original research definitions.
- The [quick start](00_QUICK_START/QUICK_START_ONE_PAGE.md) names the files and outputs for each stage.
- The [setup guide](INSTALL_INTO_YOUR_RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md) distinguishes the reference kit from a working project and answers practical questions about AI tools, access, privacy, cost, and resuming work.
- The [worked example](09_EXAMPLES/01_FIRST_RESEARCH_PROJECT.md) follows a fictional network-switch and camera question through source checks, evidence, claims, uncertainty, recommendations, and handoff. The products and source excerpts are explicitly fictional.

The README, package metadata, and citation metadata now use the GTS AI Research System Template name and current repository address. The earlier title, AI-Powered Research Starter Kit, remains identified in the README and historical records.

## Method and compatibility

The research workflow, template fields, rigor profiles, method-selection rule, and decision boundaries are unchanged. Existing research records do not require migration.

The AI guidance explains that a recorded response can be data about what an AI system said without establishing that the statement itself is true. Claims still require supporting evidence, contradictions and uncertainty remain visible, and recommendations remain separate from accepted decisions.

This version does not add an automated research runtime or claim completion of the standards-reconciliation work planned before 1.0.

## Distribution and licensing

The proprietary baseline established in v0.2.0 continues to apply. Historical v0.1.0 CC BY 4.0 distributions retain their historical licensing; their archives do not contain v0.2.1. See [LICENSE_HISTORY.md](LICENSE_HISTORY.md) and the [setup guide](INSTALL_INTO_YOUR_RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md).

## Verification and review

The repository verifier expects package version 0.2.1. The checksum manifest must include the new documentation and updated release records.

Automated checks do not resolve the manual controls recorded in [ASSURANCE.json](ASSURANCE.json): methodological validity, source-quality judgment, claim sufficiency, conflict resolution, and ethical and legal review. Those controls continue to require human judgment appropriate to each research project.
